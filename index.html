SPACE SCIENCE ACADEMY

Learn about Mars

Explore the science behind a human mission to the Red Planet.

🔴

The Red Planet

Mars appears red because iron minerals in its soil have oxidized, or rusted.

🌬️

Thin atmosphere

Mars has a very thin atmosphere, mostly carbon dioxide. Humans would need a pressurized habitat.

🫁

Oxygen

A crewed habitat needs a reliable life-support system to provide breathable air.

☢️

Radiation

Mars has less atmospheric and magnetic protection than Earth. Habitat shielding is important.

🌱

Growing food

Controlled greenhouses could help future crews produce some of their own food.

☀️

Solar energy

Solar panels can produce electricity, although dust and weather can reduce their output.

Did you know?

A Martian day, called a sol, is about 24 hours and 39 minutes long. Mission planners must account for this difference when scheduling activities.
